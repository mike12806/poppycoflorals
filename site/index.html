Hello

Poppy & Co. is a small, one-person flower studio. I design and arrange the flowers for weddings and events, working closely with each couple or host so the arrangements suit the day, the room, and the season.

Because it's just me, I take a limited number of events — which means every one gets real attention.

What I do

  • Weddings Bridal and bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, ceremony arrangements, and table flowers.
  • Showers & parties Centerpieces and welcome arrangements for bridal showers, baby showers, birthdays, and anniversaries.
  • Events Flowers for dinners, gatherings, and small celebrations, scaled to the space.
